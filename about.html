UNKNOWN ROOM • 12:00 AM

LEAVE NOW OR STAY FOREVER

It was 12:00 AM when a storm occurred as you were coming home from work. The main road was closed because of a large car collision, so you took a detour through the forest. As you drove through the dark forest, you saw a deer crossing the road, causing you to swerve. The next thing you knew, you woke up in an old-looking room with no memory of how you got there. A note was lying on the floor in front of you.

The note reads:

"LEAVE NOW OR STAY FOREVER."
