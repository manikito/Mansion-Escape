👻 GHOST CODE THE AWAKENING

UNKNOWN ROOM • 2:13 AM

You Wake Up

Your eyes slowly open. The first thing you notice is the cold.

You are lying on the wooden floor of an unfamiliar room. Rain pounds against a boarded-up window and an old light above you flickers.

Your head hurts as pieces of your memory return.

You were driving alone through the woods in the middle of the night when a deer suddenly crossed the road. You turned the steering wheel to avoid it.

Your car left the road.

Then everything went black.

You don't remember entering this house.

Somewhere outside the room, old floorboards creak. Three possible paths surround you.
