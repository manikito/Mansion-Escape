THE HOLLOW HOUSE

EVERY CHOICE HAS A CONSEQUENCE

During a cold night, you discover an abandoned house hidden beyond the trees.

The front door is already open.

Once you step inside, the door closes behind you.

There is no single path through the house. Where you go next is entirely your choice.

#game ENTER THE HOUSE
images/entrance.jpg

CHAPTER ONE

THE ENTRANCE

The front door closes behind you.

Dust floats through a thin beam of silver moonlight coming through a broken window.

In front of you are two passages.

The hallway on the left is completely dark. From somewhere inside comes the quiet sound of an old clock.

To your right, a staircase disappears into the darkness above.

#left-hall ← ENTER THE LEFT HALLWAY #stairs CLIMB THE STAIRS →

CHAPTER TWO

THE DARK HALLWAY

The floorboards creak beneath you.

Old portraits cover the walls, but every face has faded from the photographs.

Halfway down the hallway you discover two doors.

Pale light escapes from underneath the bedroom door.

Beside it, a narrow staircase leads downward toward the basement.

#bedroom OPEN THE BEDROOM #basement GO TO THE BASEMENT

CHAPTER TWO

THE STAIRCASE

Every step groans underneath you.

When you reach the second floor, the staircase behind you disappears into darkness.

Two paths remain.

An attic ladder hangs from the ceiling.

At the opposite end of the hallway stands a tall white door.

#attic CLIMB INTO THE ATTIC #white-door OPEN THE WHITE DOOR
images/bedroom.jpg

CHAPTER THREE

THE BEDROOM

The bedroom looks untouched.

An old bed stands in the middle of the room. Moonlight passes through torn curtains.

Then the bedroom door slowly closes.

You notice an open window on the far side of the room.

Behind you is another narrow door that appears to lead deeper into the house.

#escape-ending CLIMB THROUGH THE WINDOW #ghost-ending OPEN THE NARROW DOOR

CHAPTER THREE

THE BASEMENT

The basement is colder than the rest of the house.

Silver light enters through a tiny window near the ceiling.

You discover a tunnel behind a broken wall.

Nearby, another staircase continues even farther underground.

#escape-ending ENTER THE TUNNEL #lost-ending GO DEEPER BELOW

CHAPTER THREE

THE ATTIC

Boxes cover nearly the entire attic.

At the far end of the room you notice an old photograph sitting beneath the window.

Behind the boxes is a second door that should not exist from outside the house.

#ghost-ending EXAMINE THE PHOTOGRAPH #house-ending OPEN THE HIDDEN DOOR

CHAPTER THREE

THE WHITE DOOR

The room beyond the door is completely empty.

A single window fills the room with silver light.

Across from you is an exterior balcony.

But another black door stands in the corner.

#escape-ending STEP ONTO THE BALCONY #house-ending OPEN THE BLACK DOOR
